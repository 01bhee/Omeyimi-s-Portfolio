Featured Research

final-year research · Covenant University

Curriculum RL for Quadruped Terrain Adaptation

Trained a Boston Dynamics Spot quadruped to walk and adapt across terrains in MuJoCo simulation, producing one generalized policy from a progressive curriculum.

  • ▸Reward function with forward velocity, gait-phase incentives, and symmetry penalties for stable locomotion
  • ▸PPO baseline achieving a consistent diagonal trot on flat ground (~0.6 m/s)
  • ▸Curriculum pipeline progressively exposing the agent to rough, flooded, and snow terrain
  • ▸Custom MuJoCo XML scenes per terrain with unique friction values (mu)
